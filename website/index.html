I will praise you, Lord, with all my heart.Psalm 9:1, Berean Standard Bible

The game

Twelve-stage journey

Win matches to earn stars and lamps, from the Garden to the final stage. Each stage brings a new keeper and a new challenge.

Two ways to shoot

Flick the ball toward the goal, or aim and stop the timing bar. Choose the one that suits you in Settings.

Selah and the nerve meter

Pressure builds in tight matches. Take a Selah pause before a kick to steady your aim.

Fruit balls and armor

Nine balls, one for each fruit of the Spirit, and six pieces of keeper gear. Each has a benefit and a trade-off. All are unlocked by playing, never bought.

Daily Bread

One new challenge every day, the same for every player, with an optional reminder you control.

More ways to play

Friendly matches, a goalkeeper mode, precision targets, a seven-round Jericho challenge and a seven-day streak.

Everywhere you are

Eleven languages

The game and its verses are available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified) and Hebrew, with full right-to-left layouts for Arabic and Hebrew.

Also inside

An optional Bible reader

Read a full Bible in your language, with search, bookmarks and highlights. It downloads only when you first open it, and the game never needs it.

Free, with fair ads

The game is free to play. Ads support it, and a one-time purchase removes them. Nothing that affects play is ever sold. Read how ads and your data are handled in the privacy policy, or get help.
