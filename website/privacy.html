Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Tehillah Soccer has no accounts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads come from Google AdMob.

On your phone

Your settings, progress, bookmarks, downloaded Bibles and a short error log are stored on your device only. We never receive them. The error log is sent only if you choose to email it. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Ads

Ads are shown through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and collect information such as ads seen, ad interactions, approximate location from your IP address and diagnostics. See how Google uses information.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent where required and requests no ads until you answer. You can change your choice in Settings, Privacy choices. Declining does not remove ads; Google decides which ads can be shown.
  • On iOS the app asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this in iOS Settings.
  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID in system settings.

Purchases

Google Play or the App Store handles all payments. We never receive payment details.

Downloads

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. The Bible reader downloads a Bible from it when you first open the reader, and the app may read a small settings file from it at launch. Like any website, it sees your IP address when your phone asks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. This website sets no cookies.

Notifications

The Daily Bread reminder is a local notification on your phone. It is off until you turn it on.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over.
