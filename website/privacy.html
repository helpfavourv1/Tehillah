Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Applies to the Tehillah Soccer app for Android and iOS and to this website.

The short version: the game has no accounts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are shown through Google AdMob, and where the law requires it you are asked for consent first.

Who we are

Tehillah Soccer is made by an independent developer. You can contact us at stmakarios@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Your settings, such as theme, language, sound and reminders.
  • Your progress: stars, lamps, unlocks, achievements and Daily Bread history.
  • Reader bookmarks, highlights and downloaded Bibles.
  • A short diagnostic log of errors. It is sent only if you choose to email it to us from the error screen or Settings.

We have no accounts, no server copy of your data, and no analytics or crash-reporting service. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Ads and consent

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, a full-screen ad at natural breaks, and optional ads you choose to watch for a bonus. To do this, Google's advertising software on your device may use your advertising ID and collect information such as the ads you have seen, how you interact with them, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic data. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: at launch the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where required. Where a required choice has not yet been made, no ad is requested until you make it. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy choices. Declining consent does not remove ads; Google decides which ads, including limited ads without personalization, can be shown.
  • Elsewhere: no consent form is shown and ads start normally.
  • iOS: the app asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, your device identifier is not shared for advertising. You can change this in iOS Settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banner and full-screen ads. Optional ads you choose to watch remain available.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app asks the store which purchases your account owns so that Remove Ads and Restore purchase work.

Downloads from our website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The Bible reader downloads a Bible file from it the first time you open the reader, and the app may read a small settings file from it when it starts. When your phone makes these requests, the host receives your IP address and standard request information, as with any website. We do not use these requests to identify you or build a profile. Cloudflare's policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

Notifications

The Daily Bread reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. It is off until you turn it on, the app asks for permission at that moment, and no notification server is involved.

Bible texts

The Bible translations in the app are used under their licenses, which are listed under Settings, Licenses.

Children

Tehillah Soccer is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. You can delete downloaded Bibles in Settings, withdraw ad consent under Privacy choices where it appears, and remove all local data by uninstalling the app. If you believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, we update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
