Common questions

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings, then Remove Ads, and follow the store's purchase screen. It is a one-time purchase that removes banner and full-screen ads. Optional ads you choose to watch for a bonus remain available.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought with. It is always visible there.

Can I change my ad or privacy choices?

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Settings shows a Privacy choices row where you can review or change your consent. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID in the system settings. On iOS you can change tracking permission in the system settings.

How does the Bible reader download work?

Nothing is downloaded until you open the reader for the first time. You then choose Wi-Fi only, or Wi-Fi and mobile data. You can delete downloaded Bibles in Settings at any time. The game works fully without them.

Can I read the Bible in a different language from the game?

Yes. The reader language is separate from the game language. Use the language button in the reader header.

Which Bible translations are used, and under what licenses?

Every translation and its license notice is listed in Settings, then Licenses, inside the app.

How do I turn the Daily Bread reminder on or off?

Open Daily Bread or Settings and use the reminder switch. The reminder is a local notification on your phone and is off until you turn it on.

The app misbehaved. How do I report it?

The app keeps a short diagnostic log on your phone. From the error screen or Settings you can email those lines to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Matches, progress and the bundled verses work without a connection. Ads, purchases, the Bible download and the optional remote settings file need a connection.

How do I delete my data?

We keep no account or server copy of your progress. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. Downloaded Bibles can also be deleted in Settings.
